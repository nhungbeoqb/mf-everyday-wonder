Entry for the make.fun bounty **Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line** — one sentence, up to 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life.

`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The spin stops, the drum sighs, and the socks inside have paired off like strangers who met in the dark and decided to stay together.

Pure Python 3.13 standard library, deterministic — two runs produce byte-identical output.
